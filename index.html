The 40-Year Heartbeat of Povarovo

LOC: 56°5′0″N 37°6′37″E BAND: 4625 kHz SINCE: ~1973

Somewhere in the cold marshland northwest of Moscow, a relentless shortwave drone has ticked like a wounded clock every two seconds without interruption across six decades.

The Signature

A monotone buzz repeated ~25 to 30 times per minute, 24 hours a day. During rare intervals, the buzzing cuts out abruptly and cold, hurried human voices recite Russian names and military cipher strings: "Ya hydroplane 45 78 92 01."

Phonetic Cipher Key

The Dead Man's Switch Myth

Popular folklore posits it as the trigger for the Soviet Perimetr system (a nuclear retaliatory fail-safe). However, ionospheric radio engineers point to something more plausible yet equally strange: an atmospheric probe monitoring ion density over European skies.
